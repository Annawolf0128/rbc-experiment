# Reversed Beauty Contest — Design Notes

## Context

This project implements the Reversed Beauty Contest experiment as separate oTree programs for the US and Singapore laboratory sites.

## Experimental implementation

### Singapore session structure

As revised on 2026-08-21, every Singapore official session contains only one treatment. Each session recruits exactly 15 participants: a small-group treatment forms three independent groups of five, while a large-group treatment forms one group of fifteen. The earlier Singapore plan paired one 15-person group and one 5-person group in a 20-person session; that arrangement was replaced to avoid mixing group-size treatments within a session. All participant-facing pages, treatment parameters, payoff rules, survey items, payment settings, and the 20-round structure remain unchanged.

The Singapore run schedule was subsequently refined on 2026-08-21 to balance the four main no-belief blocks at three independent groups each. Small–Low and Small–High each require one session because a small-group session produces three groups of five. Large–Low and Large–High each require three sessions because a large-group session produces one group of fifteen. The two belief treatments are each run for one session only. The resulting plan has 10 sessions, 16 groups, and 150 recruited participants: 120 participants in the four main no-belief blocks and 30 participants in the belief treatments.
